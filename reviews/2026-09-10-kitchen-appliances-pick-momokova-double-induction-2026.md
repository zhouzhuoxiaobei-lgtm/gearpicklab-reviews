### Frequently Asked Questions About the MOMOKOVA Double Induction
### What kind of pots and pans work with this cooktop?
It works with cast iron. Iron, enameled iron - and stainless steel that has a magnetic bottom. A quick test is to hold a small magnet against the cookware base; if it sticks, the pan is compatible.

### Does the griddle come included?
No. The cooktop is designed to work with removable griddles that have heat-resistant rubber handles, but no griddle or baking tray is included in the box.

### How does the folding design help with storage?
The two burners fold together into a compact shape with a built-in handle. Which makes it easier to tuck away in small kitchens, dorms, or an RV. It's meant to be carried and stored rather than left out permanently.

### What power settings and timer options does it have?
You get8 power levels covering functions like melt, simmer, steam, boil, roast, stir fry - and fast heat, adjusted with plus and minus touch controls. A 99-minute timer is also built in.

### Can I use both burners at the same time?
Yes, the cooktop has two heating zones so you can cook two dishes simultaneously, such as simmering a sauce on one side while boiling pasta on the other. The total output is 1800W across both zones.
