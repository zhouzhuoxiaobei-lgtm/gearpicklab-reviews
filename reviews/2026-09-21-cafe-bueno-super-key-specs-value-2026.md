### Frequently Asked Questions About the Cafe Bueno Super
### Does this machine work with plant-based milks like oat or almond?
Yes. The milk frother is built to handle all milk types, from dairy to plant-based, so oat, almond, and soy will all froth.

### How long does it take to heat up before I can brew?
The dual thermoblock heating system reaches brewing temperature in about one minute.

### What drinks can I make with the 21 presets?
Presets cover hot lattes, cappuccinos, americano, hot water, latte, flat white, milk coffee, hot milk, ristretto, mocha, cafe macchiato, cortado, latte macchiato, hot milk foam, and some double shot options.

### Do I have to clean the milk system and brewer manually?
No. There are four self-cleaning programs - milk system clean, regular brewer clean, descaling, and deep brewer clean - each started with a button press.

### Does it grind and tamp the coffee for me. Or do I need to do that myself?
It handles both automatically. The machine grinds, tamps - and brews with a single press on the touch screen.
