Yes, the slope switch can be turned off, which makes it legal for competition golf. When slope correction is on, it provides adjusted distances for uneven terrain.

### What measurement modes does it offer?
You get six modes: slope compensation, golf flag locking, horizontal and height ranging, speed measuring, and continuous scan measurement. You cycle through them with a brief button press.
