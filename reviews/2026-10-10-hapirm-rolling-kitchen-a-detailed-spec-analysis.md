### Frequently Asked Questions About the HapiRm Rolling Kitchen
### How much weight can this cart hold?
The top surface supports up to 100 lbs. And each mesh basket holds up to 35 lbs. That's enough for a microwave, air fryer, or toaster on top and heavier items in the baskets.

### Does it have wheels, and can I lock them?
Yes, it has 4 wheels that rotate 360 degrees for easy movement. Two of the wheels lock to keep the cart from sliding when you want it stationary.

### What storage options does it offer besides the shelves?
You get 4 open shelves, a spacious tabletop, pull-out baskets on one side, and 6 S-hooks for hanging small tools like spoons, mugs, or towels. The bilateral design separates a solid wooden board side from the sliding basket side.

### Can I use this cart outside the kitchen?
Yes, it's designed for multiple uses including a bar cart, coffee cart, snack cart, office printer stand, or balcony storage rack. It works in living rooms, dining rooms, bathrooms - and pantries.

### What materials is it made from?
