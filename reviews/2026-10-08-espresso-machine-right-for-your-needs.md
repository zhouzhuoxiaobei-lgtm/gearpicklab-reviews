Everything else you need to judge this purchase is either thin or missing. So let's work with what's actually here. The 20-bar figure is the headline. Most home espresso machines run 9 to 15 bar at the group head. And 20 bar is a marketing-friendly number that tells you the pump is capable, not that your shots will taste better automatically. Pressure above roughly 9 bar at the puck doesn't improve extraction and can channel if the grind and dose aren't dialed in. So treat 20 bar as a ceiling, not a promise. The 51 oz tank is the more useful spec. That's about 1.5 liters* (based on available data). Which covers several days of espresso shots or a few pots of brewed coffee before a refill. Removable means you can carry it to the sink instead of pouring water into a reservoir in place, which matters more than people expect in a space-saving kitchen. What the machine does Two extraction paths. Espresso through the portafilter with single and double baskets included. Plus a brewed coffee mode and a built-in milk frother. For everyday listening. The frother is a steam wand or a built-in frothing element; the listing doesn't say which, and that distinction changes how much control you get over microfoam texture. The touchscreen is full-color and guided. With step-by-step prompts and real-time brew updates. For someone moving up from a drip machine. That walkthrough is the difference between using the espresso function twice and abandoning it. Manual extraction mode lets you set your own shot time. Which is the feature that actually matters if you want to tune strength rather than accept a preset. For comparison. Temperature control is described as maintaining a stable brewing environment. Cleaning is handled two ways: a one-touch high-temperature self-clean cycle and an indicator light that reminds you after every 500 cups. Five hundred cups is a long interval. If you pull two shots a day. That's roughly eight months between reminders, so the light is a backstop, not a maintenance schedule. What's in the box Portafilter, single-shot basket, double-shot basket, coffee basket, measuring scoop, cleaning needle, and the water tank. What isn't in the data No dimensions, no weight, no wattage, no housing material detail beyond "stainless steel" in the title, and no stated brew temperature. For a machine that claims barista-level temperature precision. The absence of a temperature number is the biggest hole in this listing. Who this is for Someone with limited counter space who wants to stop owning two machines. And who values a guided touchscreen to shorten the learning curve.
