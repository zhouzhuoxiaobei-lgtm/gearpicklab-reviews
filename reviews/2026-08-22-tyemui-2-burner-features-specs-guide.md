### Frequently Asked Questions About the TYEMUI 2 Burner
### Can this cooktop be used both built into a countertop and as a portable unit?
Yes. It has a flush-mount design with a specified cutout size of 19.3" x 10.6" x 2.3". And it also comes with 4 anti-slip feet for freestanding use on a counter or table.

### What kind of cookware works with this induction cooktop?
Only magnetic cookware works. Such as cast iron, stainless steel, and enameled steel. The bottom diameter must be between 4.7" and 10"; a simple magnet test confirms compatibility.

### Does this cooktop need special wiring or an electrician to install?
No. It plugs directly into a standard 110V-120V US wall outlet with the included 3-prong plug, so no hardwiring is required.

### What safety features are included?
You get a child lock. Auto shut-off - and cookware detection. The cookware detection prevents heating if no compatible pot is present. And the timer can be set from 1 to 180 minutes.

### How much power does each burner provide, and can I control them separately?
The upper zone is 1100W and the lower zone is 800W. Each with 8 power levels ranging from 100W to 1100W. The two zones are independently controlled. So you can set different heat levels on each simultaneously.
