### Frequently Asked Questions About the Wowteam Milk Frother
### How much milk can it froth versus heat at one time?
It froths up to 115ml (3.9oz) for foam and heats up to 240ml (8.2oz) for warm milk. So if you want foam, you're working with a smaller batch than if you just want hot milk.

### What are the four modes it offers?
It makes warm dense foam. Light froth, cold foam, and heated milk. That covers hot lattes and cappuccinos as well as cold foam for iced drinks.

### Does it shut off on its own?
Yes, it has an auto shut-off that stops the unit once the milk or foam reaches the intended texture. That's meant to prevent spills and burnt milk.

### How do I clean it?
The interior is non-stick and scratch-resistant, so you rinse and wipe it rather than scrub. No disassembly is mentioned in the specs.

### What is the pitcher made of and what capacity is the unit?
It's stainless steel with a 240ml (8.2oz) capacity. The 8.2oz figure matches the maximum heating volume, not the foam volume.
