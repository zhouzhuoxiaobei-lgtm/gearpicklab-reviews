The 20W and 40W trade spot size for cutting muscle, so if you mostly engrave, this is the better-balanced choice. That 24. 000 mm/min figure is a ceiling, not a working speed. Diode lasers move fast on light engraving passes and measured down dramatically when cutting, because the head has to dwell long enough to burn through material. You'll see the headline number on shallow engraving. Not on a 15 mm plywood cut. The cutting ratings deserve a second look. The 20 mm wood and 30 mm acrylic figures don't come with pass counts in the specifications. But the product video labels the acrylic result "Multiple-Pass Cutting 30mm Acrylic." Treat both as multi-pass numbers. Realistically, expect clean single-pass cuts in the 3-8 mm range on plywood and acrylic, with thicker stock requiring repeated passes, air help, and patience. For everyday listening, the video showing an 8 mm plywood cut is a more honest reference point than the 20 mm figure. The 400 × 400 mm bed is the standout for this class of engraver. The ESP32 controller's multi-machine capability is aimed at shops running several units. But for a single owner it's mostly future headroom. Limitations and data gaps The listing doesn't publish weight. Dimensions of the assembled frame, or a warranty term, so I can't tell you how portable it's or how it's supported long-term. In real-world use, the "lifetime technical service" line is a support claim, not a durability guarantee, and I'd want to see a written warranty before counting on it. The safety features - emergency stop, motion protection, overheat cutoff, vibration auto-shutdown - are listed but not specified in detail, so I can't say what triggers the motion protection or how sensitive it is. The enclosure is a protective cover, not a full Class 1 enclosure, so ventilation and eye protection still matter. That's standard for open-frame diode lasers. But worth stating plainly. Also note the listing contradicts itself on software. The features field says LaserGRBL and LightBurn; the comparison table repeatedly spells it "LaserBum & LaserBurn." Ignore the typo - the machine runs on the standard GRBL ecosystem either way. Summary The Ray5 10W suits someone who wants a large 400 × 400 mm bed for engraving and light-to-moderate cutting, and who values the fine 0.06 mm spot over raw cutting power. The 10W module handles thin stock and detailed engraving well. But the 20 mm/30 mm cutting figures are multi-pass results, not single-pass capability. If your projects lean toward thick material removal, the higher-power versions in this series are the more sensible match despite their larger spot size. Who this is for This is a fit for a hobbyist or compact-shop owner who engraves more than they cut - signage, personalized gifts, leather goods, coated tumblers - and wants the working area to handle bigger pieces without tiling. A maker running a weekend craft business, engraving cutting boards and acrylic keychains in batches on the 400 mm bed, would use most of what this machine offers. The offline TF card and App control also help if your workshop is away from your computer. Who should look elsewhere If your primary goal is cutting thick plywood or acrylic in single passes for production, the 10W module here is the wrong tool - step up to a higher-power unit in the same line. And if you need portability or a fully enclosed Class 1 unboxing, the missing weight and enclosure specs mean this isn't the one to assume fits those needs. ### Frequently Asked Questions About the LONGER Ray5 10W
### What materials can the LONGER Ray5 10W engrave and cut?
It engraves and cuts stainless steel. Metal, wood, leather, glass, acrylic - and cotton fabric. It can cut wood up to 20 mm thick and acrylic up to 30 mm thick.

### Can I use the LONGER Ray5 10W without a computer?
Yes. It supports offline operation via TF card and App control, Also, to USB and WiFi connections. It works with LaserGRBL and LightBurn software.

### How large is the engraving area on the LONGER Ray5 10W?
The working area is 400 x 400 mm (15.75 x 15.75 inches).

### What safety features does the LONGER Ray5 10W include?
You get emergency stop, motion protection, and overheat protection that automatically powers off during abnormal vibration. A protective cover, safety goggles, and active cooling system are also included.

### How precise is the LONGER Ray5 10W?
It uses a 0.06 x 0.06 mm laser spot with engraving accuracy up to 0.01 mm. The dual-beam technology and 240MHz ESP32 chip support speeds up to 24,000 mm/min.
