### Frequently Asked Questions About the Funguard Green Laser
### How accurate is the laser line over distance?
The leveling accuracy is ±3mm at 10 meters, and the working range reaches up to 30 meters. Expect the margin of error to grow the farther you project the line.

### What happens if the tool is tilted too far in self-leveling mode?
If it exceeds the self-leveling range of 4° ± 1°. The laser beam flashes as a warning. Lock the pendulum to switch to manual mode and set the cross at any angle you need.

### How long does it run on the included batteries?
The two included AA batteries support about 6 hours of continuous laser use. The unit is not rechargeable. So plan on spare AAs for longer jobs.

### How tall does the included tripod go?
The tripod adjusts from 31cm to 83cm (about 12.2 to 32.7 inches). The laser mounts directly onto it for hands-free positioning.

### Can it handle dusty or wet job sites?
It carries an IP54 rating for dust. Water, and shock resistance, plus 1-meter drop protection. That covers typical construction and DIY conditions but not submersion.
