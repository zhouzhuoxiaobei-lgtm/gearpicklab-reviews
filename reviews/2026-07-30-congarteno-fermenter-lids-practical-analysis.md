Summary This CONGARTENO Fermenter Lids kit is a basic entry point for someone who wants to try home fermentation without buying a full set. The single lid fits standard wide mouth jars, and the tinplate material works for short-term use, but it's not as resistant to rust as stainless steel alternatives. If you plan to ferment frequently or need multiple jars going at once, you will want to look at multi-pack lids with better corrosion resistance. Who this is for This kit suits someone who is just starting to ferment and only has one or two mason jars to work with. Who should look elsewhere Anyone who wants to run multiple ferments simultaneously (three or more jars) will need to buy several of these kits, and that adds up.
