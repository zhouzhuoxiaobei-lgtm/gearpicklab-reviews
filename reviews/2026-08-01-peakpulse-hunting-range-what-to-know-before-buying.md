### Frequently Asked Questions About the PEAKPULSE Hunting Range
### What is the maximum distance this rangefinder can measure?
The listed specification is 10 to 1200 yards, with ±1 yard accuracy. Note that the product title also mentions 2200 yards. but the detailed spec only confirms the 10-1200 yard range.

### What ranging modes does it support?
You get four modes: M1 (distance, angle, horizontal distance, continuous measurement), M2 (distance, angle, vertical height, continuous measurement), M3 (two-point height), and M4 (flag lock with slope compensation).

### Is it safe to use around people and pets?
Yes. The laser output is under 1mW at 905nm and meets Class I laser certification. which the manufacturer states is safe for both humans and pets.

### Can it withstand rain or dust outdoors?
You get an IP65 rating, meaning it is dust-proof and protected against water spray or low-pressure water jets. It also uses Type-C charging, so the port is covered when closed.

### Does it have magnification?
Yes, the product title indicates 8X magnification, which is typical for spotting far-away targets like golf flags or hunting objects.
