Summary The AMZCHEF Double Induction Cooktop is a solid entry-level double burner for anyone who needs two cooking zones in a portable package, with the griddle adding real versatility for breakfast or flat-top cooking. The 1800W shared power budget means you'll manage expectations on simultaneous high-heat cooking, and the magnetic-only cookware requirement is a real filter for your existing pots and pans. It suits apartment dwellers, dorm students, or anyone who wants an indoor grill option without a dedicated appliance. Skip it if you need high-power dual-zone boiling or if your cookware collection is mostly aluminum or non-magnetic stainless. Who This Is For This unit makes sense if you're cooking for one or two people and you want flexibility without permanent installation. A closer look at the data reveals that removable griddle also means you can do a full breakfast — eggs on one side, bacon on the other — without dirtying multiple pans. Who Should Look Elsewhere If you regularly cook for four or more, or you need to boil large pots of pasta and simmer a sauce at the same time, the 1800W shared cap will frustrate you.
