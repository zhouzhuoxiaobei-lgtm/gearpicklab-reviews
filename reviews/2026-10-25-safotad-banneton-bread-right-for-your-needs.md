### Frequently Asked Questions About the Safotad Banneton Bread
### What exactly comes in this kit?
You get a 9-inch round banneton, a 10-inch oval banneton, two linen liners, a dough whisk, a bread lame with five blades, a dough scraper, a bowl scraper, a silicone brush, a cleaning brush, a flour duster, 16 baking stencils, two silicone bread slings, a 27×19-inch baking mat - and a user manual.

### What are the proofing baskets made of?
Both baskets are made from Indonesian natural rattan, described as smooth, splinter-free - and chemical-free. The material is breathable, which helps wick moisture from the dough during fermentation.

### How do I clean and care for the rattan baskets?
Avoid washing them with water and keep them out of direct sunlight. After baking. Use the included brush to sweep excess flour out of the rattan grooves.

### Do I need to do anything before putting dough in the basket?
Yes, dust the basket with flour first to prevent sticking and help the dough take on the spiral pattern as it proofs.
