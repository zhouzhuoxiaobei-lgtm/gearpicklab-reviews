### Frequently Asked Questions About the eMylo Smart WiFi
### What Wi-Fi network requirements does this thermometer have?
It needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; 5 GHz isn't supported. The Wi-Fi name and password must contain only letters and numbers, and the device should be within 10 meters (32.8 feet) of the router with minimal obstructions.

### Can I use this sensor to automatically control other smart devices?
Yes. It can act as a trigger in Tuya scenes to control compatible smart devices like fans. Humidifiers, or dehumidifiers based on temperature or humidity thresholds. It also works with Alexa and Google Home for voice queries.

### What should I check if the device keeps losing its Wi-Fi connection?
Ensure the distance to the router is under 10 meters (32.8 feet). Reduce obstructions between the device and router - and check if the battery is running low, as that can affect connectivity.
