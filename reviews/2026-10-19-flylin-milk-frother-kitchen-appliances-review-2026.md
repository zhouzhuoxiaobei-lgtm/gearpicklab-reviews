At 350 ml, this is a single-serve pitcher. That's the right size for one latte or cappuccino. And it's space-saving enough to fit under most steam wands without angling awkwardly. For everyday listening, the 9.5 cm height (about 3.7 inches) means you're not fighting clearance issues on a compact machine. The trade-off is obvious: if you're making two drinks back to back, you'll be refilling and re-steaming. For a household of one or two, that's fine. For a dinner party, it's a bottleneck. The point-mouth spout is where the design earns or loses its keep. In real-world use, the welded handle is a meaningful detail too; welded handles are sturdier than riveted ones on thin-gauge pitchers, and a heat-resistant grip matters when you're holding a pitcher that's just been under 140-degree steam.
