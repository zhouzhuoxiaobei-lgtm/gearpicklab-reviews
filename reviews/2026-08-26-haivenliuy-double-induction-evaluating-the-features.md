### Frequently Asked Questions About the Haivenliuy Double Induction
### What kind of cookware can I use with this induction cooktop?
You need magnetic pots with flat bottoms, such as iron or magnetic stainless steel. Pots should be at least 30% iron; aluminium. Ceramic, copper, glass - and concave-bottomed pots won't work.

 Here is our breakdown of the Haivenliuy Double Induction - what it does well, where it falls short, and whether it fits your needs.### Can I use both burners at the same time?
Yes, the two heating zones can be used individually or simultaneously. Each burner provides 1200W. For a combined 2400W output.

### What safety features does it have?
You get a panel lock. An automatic shutoff after 60 seconds if no cookware is detected, and error message diagnostics plus low/high voltage warnings.
