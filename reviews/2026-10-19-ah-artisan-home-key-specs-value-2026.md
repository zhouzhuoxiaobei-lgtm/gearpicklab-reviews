Rattan is the traditional material for bannetons because its coiled texture holds a dusting of flour and helps the dough keep its shape as it proofs. The linen liners matter for high-hydration doughs, which can stick to bare rattan. That said, you get one liner per basket, so a wet dough that soaks through means hand-washing before the next bake. The bread sling is the piece worth calling out. Fibreglass slings let you lower a scored loaf into a hot Dutch oven without burning your forearms. And they're reusable in a way parchment isn't. If you plan to bake in a covered pot - the usual method for home sourdough - this is a genuinely useful inclusion rather than filler. What the set doesn't include is a starter culture. The name says "starter kit. " but the box gives you equipment, not the living culture itself. You'll need to grow your own from flour and water or source one separately.
