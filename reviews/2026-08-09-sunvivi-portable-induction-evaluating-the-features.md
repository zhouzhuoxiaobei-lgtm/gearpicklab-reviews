### Frequently Asked Questions About the Sunvivi Portable Induction
### Can I use different power levels on each burner at the same time?
Yes. The left zone runs up to 1000W and the right up to 800W, and each has its own independent controls, so you can set different power or temperature levels on each side simultaneously.

### What cookware works with this induction cooktop?
It requires magnetic cookware (cast iron, stainless steel, or composite bottom) with a diameter between 4.7 and 8 inches. If a pan isn't magnetic, it won't heat.

### How long can the timer run?
The standard timer goes up to 3 hours for most modes. Stew and Heat-up modes extend to 24 hours, which suits overnight slow cooking or keeping food warm.

### What temperature range can I set?
You get8 temperature levels spanning 176°F to 518°F. Plus 8 power levels from 100W up to the zone's maximum (1000W left, 800W right).

### Is the surface easy to clean after cooking?
Yes. The top is scratch-resistant black crystal glass with a smooth. Non-stick surface, so food residue wipes off quickly without scrubbing.
