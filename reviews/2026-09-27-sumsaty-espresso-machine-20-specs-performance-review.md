Most entry-level espresso machines in this class ship with reservoirs in the 1.2-1.5L* (based on available data) range, so this unit runs larger than typical. That's a meaningful convenience difference for a household with more than one coffee drinker. The 20-bar pump deserves a caveat. Espresso extraction actually happens around 9 bar; anything above that's pump headroom. Not brewing pressure. A 20-bar rating tells you the pump has capacity, but it doesn't guarantee better shots than a 15-bar machine. What actually matters for beginners is the pre-infusion feature the listing mentions, which adjusts pressure at the start of the shot to even out extraction. Combined with the 1350W heating element and the 1-3 minute heat-up time, you're getting a machine that's ready efficient and forgiving of imperfect technique. The 51mm portafilter is smaller than the 58mm* (based on available data) standard used on prosumer machines. That means fewer aftermarket baskets and accessories fit, and your puck is narrower.
