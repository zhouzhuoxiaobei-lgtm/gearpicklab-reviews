If you're meal-prepping a dozen at once, you're running two cycles, which cuts into the time savings. Firmness control works through water volume, not a dial or digital timer. For everyday listening, this is simple and solid, but it also means your results depend on measuring accurately. Eyeballing the water line will give you inconsistent eggs. The piercing pin on the measuring cup is a detail worth noting. Poking a compact hole in the shell before cooking reduces cracking and, according to the description, releases sulfur to keep yolks yellow rather than green-tinged. If you've dealt with gray-ringed hard-boiled eggs. This addresses that directly. Cleanup is straightforward. In real-world use, the tray, lid, and measuring cup are dishwasher safeguarded. That said, the listing says it works as a veggie or food steamer, but the only included tray is the 7-egg tray. There's no steamer basket in the box. You could theoretically steam small items on the tray, but the capacity and design are optimized for eggs. Treat the steamer function as a bonus at best, not a reason to buy. Space-saving design is legitimate here. A 7-egg cooker is compact enough for a dorm drawer or RV cabinet. And it draws far less counter space than a pot on a stove. Who this is for: This fits a household that eats boiled eggs several times a week - think breakfast meal prep, salads, or deviled eggs for potlucks.
