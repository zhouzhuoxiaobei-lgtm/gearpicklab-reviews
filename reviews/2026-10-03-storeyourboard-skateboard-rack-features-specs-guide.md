A flat ABS bracket has almost no resistance to bending across its span, and a loaded longboard hanging off a single wall anchor is exactly the load that bends plastic. An I-beam cross-section adds stiffness in the direction the weight pulls, which is why this design can credibly claim three boards without a metal frame.
