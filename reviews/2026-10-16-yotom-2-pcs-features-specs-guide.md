Summary This is a entry-level analog dial thermometer sold in pairs, with a 2-inch face, a 2.2-inch probe, and a stated ±10°F tolerance. It suits charcoal and gas cooks who want a rough chamber reading and a visual zone reference without spending on a digital probe configuration. The limited probe length and wide tolerance mean it's less suited to long. Low smokes where small temperature drift compounds over hours. Anyone needing tighter control should look at digital multi-probe units instead. Who this is for You're cooking on a charcoal kettle.
