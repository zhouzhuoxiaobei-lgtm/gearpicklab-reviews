If you're doing more than entry-level "is this live?" checks. You'll need a different tool. The storage case is a blow-molded plastic shell with cutouts for each tool. In real-world use, the downside is that the blow-molded cases don't age gracefully - the hinges and latches are the first thing to go, and once they break, the case becomes a loose pile of tools rattling around your trunk.
