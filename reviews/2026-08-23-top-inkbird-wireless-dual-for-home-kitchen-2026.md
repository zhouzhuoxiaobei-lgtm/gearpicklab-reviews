### Frequently Asked Questions About the INKBIRD Wireless Dual
### How does the thermometer switch between Bluetooth and WiFi connections?
At home, it uses Bluetooth with a range of up to 305 meters. When you're outdoors. It automatically switches to WiFi (5G or 2.4G) so you can keep monitoring remotely.

### What do the two probes measure - and how accurate are they?
Each probe has 4 food sensors and 1 ambient sensor. So they measure both the food's internal temperature and the oven or grill temperature. The food sensors are accurate to ±0.3°C/±0.5°F, while the ambient sensors are accurate to ±5.0°C/±9.0°F.

### Can I leave the probes in the dishwasher after cooking?
Yes. The probes are made of food-grade stainless steel with zirconia ceramic handles, are IP67 waterproof, and are explicitly rated as dishwasher-safe.

### What happens if my phone dies or the network drops mid-cook?
The base unit stores up to 60 minutes of offline temperature data. Once the connection is restored, it automatically syncs the full temperature curve to your phone.

### How long does the battery last - and how long does it take to recharge?
Each probe lasts 25 hours of continuous cooking, which covers an 8-hour sous-vide session or all-day smoking. Recharging takes just 25 minutes.
