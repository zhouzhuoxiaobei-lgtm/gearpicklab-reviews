### Frequently Asked Questions About the Nikon COOLSHOT PROII
### How much does the stabilization actually reduce shake?
Nikon rates the STABILIZED function as reducing shake by roughly 80 percent, which steadies both the viewfinder image and the ranging laser. That matters most when you're trying to lock onto a small flag rather than a large background object.

### How do I know the device is measuring the flag and not a tree behind it?
The Dual Locked On Echo system shows a LOCKED ON SIGN in the viewfinder and plays an electronic sound when it's ranging the nearest object. That gives you both a visual and audio confirmation before you trust the number.

### How fast does it return a distance reading?
The HYPER READ laser returns a measurement in 0.3 seconds or less, and Nikon says the response stays quick and stable regardless of distance.

### Does it account for uphill and downhill shots?
Yes, the ID Technology gives you a single adjusted number for incline or decline angles. The device also has a non-tilt D mode for straight-line distance, which keeps it usable in competitions that don't allow slope-adjusted readings.

### Can I use it in rain or fog?
The unit is waterproof and fogproof, so wet or misty conditions shouldn't be a problem. The OLED display also dims automatically based on surrounding light.
