### Frequently Asked Questions About the Rice Cooker &
### How many people will this rice cooker actually serve?
The 5.5-cup uncooked capacity is designed for 1 to 5 people. That makes it workable for a small family or a gathering. But it's not built for large batches.

### Can I cook rice and steam vegetables at the same time?
Yes. The included steaming basket lets you steam food in the same pot while the rice cooks below. So you can put together a one-pot meal in a single run.

### How does the keep-warm function work?
There's a one-button control with an automatic keep-warm mode that holds rice at serving temperature after cooking. The specs note keeping it warm for 10 minutes gives better taste. So it's meant for short holding rather than all-day storage.

### What comes in the box?
You get the rice cooker itself plus a measuring cup. A spatula, and a steamer basket. The inner pot and inner lid are removable. And there's a condensation collector as well.

### Is cleanup difficult?
The non-stick inner pot. Detachable inner lid, and condensation collector all come out for cleaning, and the surfaces can be wiped down with a wet cloth. No parts are listed as dishwasher-safe. So hand washing is the safe assumption.
