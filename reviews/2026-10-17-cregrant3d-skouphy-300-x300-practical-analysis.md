we noticed the black oxide coating is the detail worth understanding. Bare metal honeycomb reflects laser light back up at the module. And that reflected energy is wasted at best and a wear factor at worst. A dark, oxidized surface absorbs more of it, which is why the coating is there. If you're running a diode laser at higher power, this is the kind of compact thing that keeps the module running cooler over a long session. The ruler and fixing pins address the mundane but constant chore of squaring material. In everyday use, a X/Y scale forming a right angle means you can line up a workpiece against the grid instead of measuring and marking each time. Ten pins hold thin stock flat so it doesn't shift mid-cut - useful when you're running a batch of miniature identical pieces and can't afford a nudge between passes. For everyday listening, the aluminum panel underneath is the part that protects your desk, not your laser. It catches the beam on through-cuts so it doesn't burn the table below. Four corner protectors are a sensible addition given the exposed edges of a steel grid, though the listing doesn't specify the steel gauge or the bed's weight, so you can't judge how much flex to expect across that 300 mm span. Data gaps are real here. There's no listed thickness, weight, or steel grade for the honeycomb panel, no stated maximum temperature despite the "heat-resistant" label, and no compatibility detail beyond "ACMER S1 and most laser engravers within size." The "within size" caveat is doing a lot of work - confirm your machine's working area is at or under 300 x 300 mm, because a larger bed won't fit and a smaller one wastes footprint. The listing is also categorized under Toys & Games, which tells you nothing useful about the product; ignore it. In real-world use, this suits someone running a portable diode or entry-level laser with a cut area around 10 inches square who's tired of scorched edges and manual squaring - hobbyists cutting space-saving wood pieces in batches, or anyone who wants a stable, aligned surface without building one. If you're running a larger-format machine, cutting mostly paper or card where smoke under the work isn't an issue, or you need published thickness and load specs before buying, this one leaves you guessing. ### Frequently Asked Questions About the Cregrant3D Skouphy 300 x300
### What size materials can I cut or engrave on this honeycomb bed?
The working area is 10.24 x 10.24 inches (300 x 300 mm). Anything larger won't fit within the bed's footprint.

 If you are deciding on the Cregrant3D Skouphy 300 x300, this breakdown covers the details that usually decide the purchase.### Will this work with my laser engraver, or is it only for the ACMER S1?
It's designed for the ACMER S1 but the listing says it fits most laser engravers within the 300 x 300 mm size. Check your machine's footprint against that working area before buying.

### Does it actually protect my desk from the laser?
Yes, it comes with an aluminum panel underneath the honeycomb to shield your desk from laser damage, plus four corner protectors for your hands.

### How do I hold materials in place while engraving?
The package includes 10 fixing pins for securing your material. The X and Y axes also have scale lines forming a right-angle ruler so you can measure and align cuts.

### What does the black oxide coating do?
The oxidation process adds a black coating that reduces light reflection and protects the laser module. The open honeycomb design also improves airflow so smoke clears faster and materials are less likely to darken or warp.
