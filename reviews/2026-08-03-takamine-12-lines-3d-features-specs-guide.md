Burst flash mode makes the laser line flash so it's easier to spot in bright sunlight, which helps with outdoor work. You can turn it on or off using the separate H and V buttons on the keypad.

Yes. The included patented bracket can be fixed to a wall using magnets or screws, and the base rotates 360° for angle adjustment. It also has 1/4"-20 and 5/8"-11 threads if you prefer a tripod or mounting pole.
