### Does the laser level come with a receiver for outdoor use?
No. The pulse mode extends range to 165ft, but it requires a laser receiver that must be purchased separately. Without the receiver, pulse mode isn't useful.

### How does the self-leveling work, and what happens if the surface is too tilted?
In self-leveling mode, the tool levels itself when it's within ±4° of horizontal. If the surface tilts more than 4 degrees, the lasers blink and the LED light on top goes red.

Yes. Slide the switch to the lock side and press the button for 3 seconds to turn on manual mode, which disables self-leveling so you can lock lines to other angles.
