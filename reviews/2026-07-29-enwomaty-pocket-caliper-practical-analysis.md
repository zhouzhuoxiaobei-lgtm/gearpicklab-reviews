Looking at the data, sliding action on a brass body can feel smooth out of the box, but brass-on-brass can develop play over time. What the specifications tell us is that the measuring range (100mm) limits you to small objects. Capable for jewelry, compact electronics components, model-making. Not suitable for larger workpieces like pipe diameters or wooden dowels over about 4 inches. Based on the available information, caliper also only measures internal and external diameters and length—there’s no depth rod mentioned, so you can’t measure the depth of a hole or recess. Customer reviews hover around 3.8 stars out of 5. For a basic brass caliper under ten dollars online, that rating aligns with what you’d expect. Summary The Enwomaty Pocket Caliper is a functional entry-level brass vernier for measuring small objects up to 100mm. It offers dual scales in mm and inches, a small build, and a material that beats plastic tools. But it lacks stated accuracy, a locking mechanism, and depth measurement—limitations that matter if you need repeatable precision. Ratings suggest it’s acceptable for hobby use but not for key work. Who this is for This caliper suits anyone who requires to quickly measure the outside or inside diameter of small items—ring sizes, bead holes, wire gauges, gemstone diameters, or model parts—without worrying about tenths of a millimeter. If you’re a jewelry maker, crafter, or hobbyist who values portability and a tool that doesn’t feel cheap, this is a reasonable choice. The brass body gives it a satisfying weight and classic look that plastic alternatives lack.
