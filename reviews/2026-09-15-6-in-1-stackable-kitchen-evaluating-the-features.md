The material choice deserves a straight look. PS plastic - polystyrene - is rigid and budget to mold, which is why it shows up in budget kitchen gadgets. It's food-secure and the smooth surface does make wiping it down quick. What PS isn't is heat-tolerant or impact-resistant the way polypropylene or silicone tends to be. A peeler or can opener blade backed by PS plastic is fine for routine use; the plastic housing is where you'd expect wear to show first if these tools get banged around in a drawer or run through hot water repeatedly.
