### Frequently Asked Questions About the VEOFEMLEA 30-Bin Wall
### How many bins come in this kit, and what sizes are they?
You get 30 bins total: 6 small red, 6 small yellow, 6 small orange, 6 large green, and 6 large blue. That works out to 18 small bins and 12 large bins.

### Can I mount the two backboards separately or do they've to go up together?
Either way works. The two backboards can be installed separately on the wall, or you can join them with the 4 included H-shape links to form one larger unit.

### What mounting hardware is included?
The kit comes with 2 mounting boards, 4 H-shape links, 8 wall fixing screws, and 2 stickers. You don't need to buy separate brackets to hang it.

### Do the bins have lids?
No, they're lid-free, so you can see the contents at a glance. That's handy for screws and bolts, but it also means small parts are exposed to dust.

### What are the bins made of?
The bins are made from ABS plastic, which the listing describes as strong and durable. No wall-mount weight limit is given in the specs.
