### Frequently Asked Questions About the KEENSTAR 20Bar Espresso
### How long does the cold brew take?
Cold brew is ready in as little as 10 minutes, according to the manufacturer. The machine uses medium-ground coffee for cold brew. With a grind size above 0.5 mm.

### How much water does the tank hold?
The detachable water tank holds 37 ounces, which the listing says is enough to brew multiple cups before refilling.

### Do I need to do anything special before using the steam wand?
Yes. The manufacturer says to purge air from the steam wand before each use to ensure proper operation.

### What happens if I don't use the machine for a long time?
You'll need to prime the pump before the next use. The process is: with the machine off, turn the knob left about 100 degrees (not to the end), power on, press Cold Brew, wait for a thin water flow, press Cold Brew again, then turn the knob to OFF.

### What does the warranty cover?
The machine comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee, free replacement within one year, and lifetime after-customer service from the buy date.
