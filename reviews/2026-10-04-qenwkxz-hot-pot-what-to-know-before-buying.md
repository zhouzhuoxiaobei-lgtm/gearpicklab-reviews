The double-layer body addresses a real complaint with inexpensive electric pots: a hot exterior shell that you can't touch. That matters if you're carrying a full pot of boiling broth from a counter to a desk or bed. The overheat and boil-dry protections cut power when the pot runs empty or gets too hot - useful if you walk away and forget about it, which is straightforward to do with a cooker this size. The non-stick coating is ceramic glaze over stainless steel. Ceramic non-stick releases food cleanly and doesn't use the PTFE coatings some people avoid, but it's also more fragile than traditional non-stick if you scrape it with metal utensils. Use silicone or wood and it should hold up; attack it with a fork and the coating will score.
